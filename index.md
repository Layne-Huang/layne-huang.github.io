<section class="intro-panel" id="about">
  <p class="eyebrow">AI for drug design | Generative biology | Single-cell genomics</p>
  <h2>Developing generative AI for molecules, proteins, DNA, and single-cell systems.</h2>
  <p>
    I am a Research Fellow in Prof. <a href="http://ec2-3-220-229-138.compute-1.amazonaws.com/people/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Luca Pinello's</a>
    group at Massachusetts General Hospital and Harvard Medical School. I received my PhD from the Department of Computer Science,
    City University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. <a href="http://www.cs.toronto.edu/~wkc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ka-Chun Wong</a>,
    after completing my bachelor's degree at Wuhan University.
  </p>
  <p>
    My research builds precise and robust AI models for high-throughput drug discovery, spanning de novo molecular design,
    biological activity prediction, drug-target interaction annotation, and single-cell omics generation and analysis. During
    my visiting scholar appointments with Prof. <a href="http://compbio.mit.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Manolis Kellis</a>
    at MIT and Prof. <a href="https://zitniklab.hms.harvard.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Marinka Zitnik</a> at Harvard,
    I worked on generative models for single-cell genomics and disease-relevant biomarker discovery.
  </p>
